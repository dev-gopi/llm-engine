# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free
experience for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different perspectives.
- Focus feedback on ideas and work, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and help make participation accessible.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
or sharing others' private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and take other appropriate action in response to
behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.
